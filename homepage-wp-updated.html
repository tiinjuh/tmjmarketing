How it works

Week 1 — Define one goal

We start by getting clear on what actually matters. In week one I conduct an initial audit — reviewing your current marketing, talking to your sales team, and analysing your pipeline, CRM, and channels. Together we agree on one clear, measurable commercial goal. Everything we build is in service of that goal, not a generalised marketing plan.

Months 1–2 — Build the system

I design and build the marketing, sales enablement, and AI automation infrastructure needed to hit the goal. This typically includes demand creation (content, SEO, paid, LinkedIn), sales enablement (buyer signals, prospect research, CRM enrichment), and the automation workflows that connect both. Weekly alignment with your sales team ensures we stay focused on what's actually moving pipeline.

Month 3 — Measure and optimise

At 60–90 days we have real data. We review what's working, double down on the highest-impact activities, and cut what isn't moving the needle. A monthly performance review with clear metrics — pipeline contribution, not impressions — gives us the basis for a sharp scale-up plan. By this point the system is running, the metrics are clear, and we know exactly what to do more of.

Ongoing — Scale what works

Beyond 90 days, the focus shifts to scaling the commercial engine we've built. This means expanding what's working, adding new channels and automations, building out a full-year content and campaign plan, and continuously improving the AI workflows. The goal is a self-improving system that keeps your sales team fed with qualified pipeline — month after month.
